Build the flagship multilingual expense, income and budget product with trusted ledger mutations and auditable AI proposals.

1. Read BRIEF.md, then copy this pack into your own repository.

5. Follow milestones in the academy; save evidence in EVIDENCE.md. Checklist flags are self-reported. Do not use real private/company/financial data in these synthetic fixtures.
6. Submit a fixed repository revision in the project workspace. Record commands/output and honest limitations, invite a registered reviewer and revise after feedback.

The academy never executes this pack or fetches your repository. Use separately configured local databases/provider accounts; never copy academy credentials. Actual PostgreSQL concurrency, broker/provider behavior, language quality and release/recovery require your measured evidence and reviewer judgment.
