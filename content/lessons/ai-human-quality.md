Objective checks and semantic review answer different questions. The local harness can verify that a quoted span belongs to the current allowed source and that a note has the expected receipt. It cannot decide whether the source is true, the answer is useful or the model made a good plan. The ordinary and malicious-source answers therefore keep explicit semantic reviews pending even when all finite contract checks pass.

A HumanReview names the case, exact dataset and candidate digests, reviewer, self/human source, approve/reject verdict and a bounded explanation. These are reported judgments. The local package does not authenticate the reviewer or calibrate their expertise. The in-app project workflow separately records account-owned self review and invited human feedback against fixed submitted versions.

A review of a changed candidate is stale. The package rejects a reused digest, duplicate review or review for an unflagged case. One completed review does not fill in another missing one. Manual rejection blocks the fixture decision even if objective checks pass. Manual approval cannot override a permission leak or missing-case failure. Keep these channels visible instead of combining them into a misleading universal readiness score.
