# ADR 0040: Pinned controlled release evidence and separate quality review

Date: 2026-10-12

Status: Accepted

## Context

Published model/retrieval/RAG/tool labs verify finite local contracts. The final fixed milestone needs versioned adversarial release cases, explicit metrics/thresholds, privacy, human-quality separation and a full connected-path audit. The original product curriculum remains broader than the nine agreed teaching milestones.

## Decision

Publish academy-ai-evaluation-lab==0.1.0, depending on the completed tools package and composing all five AI packages outside the academy runtime. Add six lessons/twelve scenarios under a separate skill, complete v26 layers, a fifteen-file authenticated starter/three functions and four manual criteria. Load the strict packaged ai-release-fixture-v1 development resource on demand; require all eighteen scenarios and retain mandatory semantic-review flags.

Observe four retrieval, seven RAG and seven tool cases with actual prior operations, controlled providers and per-case owned temporary storage. Compare raw-cosine-v1 and the explicitly limited task-gated-v1. Preserve actual source/embedding model/version/dimensions and model/prompt/schema identities, request charges/counts, effect/replay and current-boundary facts; redact source/query/arguments/private exceptions. The demo executes thirty-six observations and separately labels an observation mutation as simulated regression.

Use exact Fraction arithmetic: requested-k macro precision over all retrieval cases, positive-label recall/MRR, separate unlabelled unwanted hits, expected answer/tool contracts and independent permission/privacy/boundary failures. Pin the canonical dataset digest in ai-gate-v1, require complete unique same-candidate observation coverage and apply explicit inclusive development thresholds. The task gate retains a visible threshold miss; an objective pass is not semantic accuracy.

Keep HumanReview reported data separate: case, dataset/candidate digests, reviewer/source/verdict/note. Reject stale/duplicate/unexpected records; missing reviews remain pending, rejection blocks and approval cannot override objective failure. The package does not authenticate a reviewer or calibrate expertise. The demo invents no reviews. A malicious source may be faithfully quoted without gaining capabilities, while semantic safety remains unresolved.

Audit all published layers/files/prerequisites/project manifests and compare earlier definitions to a saved baseline. Exercise every authenticated lesson/private activity and all registered starter ZIPs. Preserve learner rows and fixed artifacts. After verification close the nine fixed milestones and stop before step seven. Keep broader expert/product curriculum open in ROADMAP and steps seven/eight.

## Evidence

54 installed/source evaluation cases and prior 84 model/83 retrieval/70 RAG/81 tool cases pass in a clean five-wheel installation with quiet typed resources and saved JSON readback. Raw similarity has one unwanted unlabelled hit; the task gate has zero. Precision is 1/4, positive recall/MRR 1/2, with two semantic reviews pending. Denied socket-connect testing observes zero network attempts in the controlled matrix. All 195 lesson routes, 29 exact ZIPs, baseline definitions/state, production build/lint and six browser journeys are verified; full details and corrections are in PROGRESS/COVERAGE_AUDIT.

## Consequences and limits

Hashes identify data; they do not authenticate reported observations, manual reviewers or artifacts by themselves. Hand vectors, lexical terms and controlled providers are development tools, not held-out model/generalization or universal injection/SSRF/privacy proof. Metadata may be sensitive in production. Current authority/cross-store race/local SQLite replay/cooperative deadline/fictional accounting retain prior limitations. No real provider, network tool, judge model, learner API execution or automatic mastery is added. Closing step six records delivered bounded engineering scope; the original full curriculum, comprehensive portfolio exams and everyday operational reliability remain planned.
